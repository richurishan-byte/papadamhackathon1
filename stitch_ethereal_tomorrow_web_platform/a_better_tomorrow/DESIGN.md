## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ethereal sanctuary—a serene vision of paradise at dawn where transcendent natural beauty converges with visionary digital architecture. It evokes an uplifting, sacred, and deeply inspiring emotional landscape: breath, stillness, radiance, and limitless possibility. 

The aesthetic is characterized as **Ethereal Luminous Glassmorphism with Editorial Grace**. It balances:
- **Atmospheric Luminosity:** Diffuse golden radiance, morning-mist opacity, and soft chromatic shifts reminiscent of first light breaking through high clouds.
- **Organic Sanctuary Minimalism:** Expansive spatial volumes, generous negative space, and gentle transitions that eliminate digital friction and visual tension.
- **Divine Precision:** Translucent frosted surfaces, whispering golden hairline contours, and balanced hierarchy that feels both ancient and futuristic.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic aura of dawn breaking over an unblemished landscape: warm sunlight, pale skies, gentle flora, and grounded earth.

- **Primary (`#D6B978` - Champagne Gold):** The divine catalyst. Applied to focal calls to action, active indicators, halo highlights, and luminous accents. Supported by Golden Glow (`#F5E6C8`) for ambient radiance and hover washes.
- **Secondary (`#DCEAF4` - Heavenly Sky Blue):** The atmosphere. Utilized for ethereal tinting, backdrop ambient diffusions, secondary callouts, and serene pill elements.
- **Tertiary (`#A8BBA3` - Soft Sage):** The organic spirit. Denotes nature, renewal, peaceful states, success indicators, and secondary illustrative details.
- **Neutral (`#263D32` - Deep Forest Green):** The grounding anchor. Used strictly for high-contrast primary typography, icons, and critical structural anchors, preventing the luminous palette from feeling washed out.
- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F0`)
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Canvas: Soft Cream (`#F2EBDD`)
  - Translucent Overlay Base: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with varying opacities (`0.4` to `0.85`)
- **Semantic Mappings:**
  - Informational / Floating State: Heavenly Sky (`#DCEAF4`)
  - Harmonious Success: Soft Sage (`#A8BBA3`)
  - Luminous Warning / Sacred Alert: Warm Amber (`#E29D52`)
  - Error: Gentle Rose Ochre (`#C26D66`)

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the poetic, editorial grandeur of Playfair Display with the pristine, modernist clarity of Manrope.

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the lyrical voice of dawn and transcendence. Headings adopt gentle italicization for evocative emphasis or narrative intros. Letterforms remain spacious and uncrowded.
- **Interface & Content (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean optical rhythm, ensuring elevated legibility across long prose, labels, and microcopy against translucent glass backgrounds.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-labels:** Rendered in uppercase Manrope with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to confer an architectural, refined balance across cards and headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is defined by deep spatial breathing room and fluid, unhurried progression.

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, moving to 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile devices.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Vertical spacing leans on rhythmic expansion (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between narrative sections. Negative space is treated as an active design material that creates calm and cognitive ease.
- **Responsive Adaptability:**
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* Single-column storytelling stacks, bottom-sheet cards, condensed margins (`1.25rem`), and floating ambient glass bars.
  - *Tablet (768px – 1024px):* Staggered asymmetrical double columns, soft horizontal gutters (`2rem`).
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* Expansive scenic canvas with max container constraints (`1320px`), floating glass panels, and side-aligned ambient breathing zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows. Elevation is achieved through soft radiant glows, frosted light refraction, and subtle gold-tinted diffusion.

- **Surface Layer 0 (Atmosphere):** Base Ivory canvas (`#FAF8F0`) augmented with subtle, radial ambient gradients blending into Soft Cream (`#F2EBDD`) and Heavenly Sky Blue (`#DCEAF4`).
- **Surface Layer 1 (Glassmorphic Pane):** Translucent pure white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` to `rgba(250, 248, 240, 0.75)`) backed by a progressive backdrop blur of `16px` to `24px`. Enclosed by an ultra-thin champagne hairline border (`rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.35)`).
- **Surface Layer 2 (Raised Sanctuary Modal & Card):** Backdrop blur of `32px` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`. Border highlight: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` on top-left edge, `rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.4)` on bottom-right edge.
- **Luminous Shadow Physics:**
  - *Subtle Glow:* `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.15)`
  - *Floating Elevation:* `0 20px 48px -10px rgba(38, 61, 50, 0.06), 0 0 24px 2px rgba(245, 230, 200, 0.45)`
  - *Radiant Focus:* `0 0 0 1px #D6B978, 0 0 28px rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.4)`

## Shapes

The design uses organic curves and pill silhouettes that evoke natural forms like smooth riverstones, morning dew, and gentle celestial arches.

- **Primary Forms:** Large architectural panels and floating glass cards utilize `rounded-2xl` (`1.5rem`) to `rounded-3xl` (`2rem`).
- **Controls & Micro-elements:** Buttons, pills, status chips, search containers, and tags adopt continuous curvature (`rounded-full` / pill-shaped) to reinforce warmth, gentleness, and tactile approachability.
- **Detail Transitions:** Dividers and progress trackers utilize rounded caps and soft tapered terminators rather than blunt geometric lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Fully rounded pill silhouette. Base fill: Champagne Gold (`#D6B978`) to Golden Glow (`#F5E6C8`) linear gradient (135deg). Label color: Deep Forest Green (`#263D32`) at `fontWeight: 600`. Resting shadow: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.35)`. Hover transition: subtle scale (`1.02`), bloom increase (`rgba(245, 230, 200, 0.6)`).
- **Secondary Ethereal Outline:** Frosted pill with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`, backdrop blur of `12px`, and a border of `1.5px solid rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.5)`. Text in Deep Forest Green. Hover: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
- **Tertiary Ghost:** Pure text accompanied by a trailing delicate icon or glowing ray bullet. Accentuated with a hairline underline appearing on hover.

### Glassmorphic Cards
- Multi-layered frosted containers. Top-left light catch rendered via `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`. Outer boundary: `1px solid rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.25)`.
- Content padding set generously at `space-lg` to allow narrative breathing space.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- Slender pill profiles (`height: 28px` - `32px`). Backed by semi-translucent Sky Blue (`rgba(220, 234, 244, 0.6)`) or Warm Ivory with gold borders (`rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.4)`).
- Label typography: `label-sm` or `label-md` in Deep Forest Green (`#263D32`), accompanied by an optional glowing amber particle indicator (`6px` circular dot with soft blur).

### Input Fields
- Enclosed within frosted cream glass (`rgba(242, 235, 221, 0.5)`). Height of `52px` with full pill or `rounded-2xl` contour.
- Hairline border of `1px solid rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.3)`. Text input in Deep Forest Green; placeholder styled in muted sage-green (`rgba(38, 61, 50, 0.45)`).
- Active Focus state: transforms background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, perimeter glow in Champagne Gold (`0 0 0 2px #D6B978, 0 0 16px rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.25)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radios are concentric circular lenses: outer border in Champagne Gold, inner selected state displaying a glowing golden core with soft radial diffusion.
- Checkboxes feature soft pill-rounded corners (`rounded-md`, `6px`) in lieu of hard squares, with a clean Deep Forest Green icon over a golden dawn wash.

### Lists & Dividing Lines
- Items segmented via ethereal horizontal gradient lines that fade to transparent at the outer boundaries (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.25) 50%, transparent)`).
- List items feature hover states that reveal an ultra-soft white glass shelf with `0 4px 16px rgba(214, 185, 120, 0.1)`.

### Ambient Ray Overlays & Particle Lights (System Signature Component)
- Decorative non-blocking background elements providing slow-moving, subtle angular light curtains (`linear-gradient(115deg, rgba(255, 246, 224, 0.25), transparent 70%)`) and microscopic ambient golden glimmer nodes to enrich the feeling of a living dawn.
